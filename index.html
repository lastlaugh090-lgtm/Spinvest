Terms

1. Service

Spinvest provides in-app NFT-style access passes, optional task rewards, and an investment-style balance tool. Spinvest is not a bank, microfinance bank, or licensed collective investment scheme unless separately stated with regulator approval.

2. NFTs

NFTs on Spinvest are digital access records themed after real-world value ideas (e.g. silver, gold, oil, property, Bitcoin). They are not blockchain tokens, not physical commodities, and carry no guarantee of resale or market value.

3. Rewards & credits

Task rewards, referral bonuses, promotional credits, and daily percentage credits are discretionary platform features. Rates may change. Past display of a rate is not a promise of future performance.

4. Withdrawals

Withdrawals are subject to minimum amounts, weekly caps, identity/bank checks, and processing times. Weekly caps are designed so large balances may take several weeks to fully withdraw.

5. User duties

You must provide accurate information, not abuse referrals or tasks, and not use the service for unlawful activity.

6. Liability

To the fullest extent permitted by law, Spinvest is not liable for indirect losses, lost profits, or delays caused by banks, networks, or third parties.
